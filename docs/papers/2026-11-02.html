Q1 · Polity & Governance · Medium

The Centre recently released states an additional instalment of tax devolution of over Rs 1.09 lakh crore. In this context, consider the following statements regarding tax devolution to states in India:

  1. The share of central taxes devolved to states is decided by the Finance Commission constituted under Article 280 of the Constitution.
  2. The 15th Finance Commission recommended that 41% of the net proceeds of central divisible pool taxes be devolved to states.
  3. Tax devolution to states is distinct from, and in addition to, grants-in-aid provided under Article 275.
  4. The vertical devolution share recommended by the Finance Commission is legally binding on Parliament and cannot be altered during annual budgeting.

How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(c) Statements 1-3 are correct: the Finance Commission is constituted under Article 280 every five years, the 15th Finance Commission recommended a 41% vertical devolution share (reduced from 42% after J&K's reorganisation into Union Territories), and tax devolution is distinct from Article 275 grants-in-aid. Statement 4 is incorrect since Finance Commission recommendations are advisory in nature and not constitutionally binding on Parliament, though conventionally accepted.

Q2 · Economy · Medium

India's October GST collections grew sharply, drawing attention to the GST framework. Consider the following statements about the Goods and Services Tax (GST) regime in India:

  1. The GST Council is constituted under Article 279A of the Constitution and is chaired by the Union Finance Minister.
  2. GST collections comprise Central GST (CGST), State GST (SGST), Integrated GST (IGST) and a Compensation Cess on select goods.
  3. Under the destination-based consumption principle, IGST revenue on inter-state trade is retained by the exporting state rather than the importing state.
  4. The current GST rate structure has only two slabs of 5% and 18%, with no additional rate for any category of goods.

How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(b) Statements 1 and 2 are correct: the GST Council is a constitutional body under Article 279A chaired by the Union Finance Minister, and GST revenue is split across CGST, SGST, IGST and Compensation Cess. Statement 3 is wrong because GST is destination-based — IGST accrues to the importing/consuming state, not the exporting state. Statement 4 is wrong because the recent rate rationalisation retained a higher 40% slab for select demerit and luxury goods alongside the two main slabs.

Q6 · Science & Technology · Hard

With reference to the legal requirement for operating wireless transmitting devices such as walkie-talkies in India, which one of the following best describes the regulatory position?

  1. Any wireless communication device can be freely operated without authorization once legally purchased, since ownership itself constitutes permission to transmit.
  2. Operating a wireless transmitting device requires a licence or authorization under the Indian Telegraph/Wireless Telegraphy framework administered by the Wireless Planning and Coordination (WPC) Wing of the Department of Telecommunications, except in specific license-exempt frequency bands.

Answer

(b) The correct position is option 2: the WPC Wing of the Department of Telecommunications administers spectrum allocation and equipment licensing under the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885 and the Indian Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1933, with certain low-power, short-range bands kept license-exempt.
