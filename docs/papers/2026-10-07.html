Q11 · Science & Technology · Hard

A tusker in Keralam died after being struck by lightning during a thunderstorm. With reference to the atmospheric phenomenon of lightning, which one of the following best describes its formation?

  1. Collisions between ice crystals and soft hail (graupel) within cumulonimbus clouds cause charge separation, which is neutralised through a sudden electrical discharge.
  2. Friction between stratus clouds and rising warm air currents during the retreating monsoon generates the electrical charge that produces lightning.
  3. Positively charged ions from the ionosphere discharge directly toward negatively charged particles accumulated at the Earth's surface.
  4. The piezoelectric effect generated by hailstones falling through the troposphere is the accepted mechanism for lightning formation.
Answer

(a) Lightning forms inside tall cumulonimbus clouds when collisions between rising ice crystals and falling graupel separate charge, building up a strong electric field that discharges as lightning. Stratus clouds are low, stable clouds not associated with thunderstorms; the ionosphere plays no direct role in cloud-to-ground lightning; and the piezoelectric effect is not the scientifically accepted mechanism.

Tests the physics of thunderstorm electrification and cumulonimbus cloud formation, a recurring UPSC theme on atmospheric phenomena and disaster-related science

Q13 · Geography · Easy

With reference to the Southwest Monsoon and its effect on Tamil Nadu's climate, consider the following statements:

  1. Tamil Nadu receives the bulk of its annual rainfall from the Northeast (retreating) monsoon rather than the Southwest monsoon, because the Western Ghats block southwest monsoon winds before they reach much of the State.
  2. The Southwest monsoon over India is driven by the seasonal reversal of winds caused by differential heating of land and the surrounding oceans.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer

(c) The Western Ghats act as an orographic barrier, creating a rain-shadow effect over most of Tamil Nadu during the Southwest monsoon, so the State depends more on the Northeast monsoon for its rainfall, making the first statement correct. The Southwest monsoon is indeed driven by differential heating between landmass and ocean causing seasonal wind reversal, making the second statement correct as well.
