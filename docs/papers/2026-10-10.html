The Chief Economic Adviser has called for aligning loan-overdue classification norms for MSMEs with their cash-flow cycles. With reference to the Reserve Bank of India's asset classification norms, consider the following statements: How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. An account is classified as a Special Mention Account-0 (SMA-0) if payment is overdue for up to 30 days.
  2. SMA-2 classification covers accounts that are overdue between 61 and 90 days.
  3. An account automatically becomes a Non-Performing Asset (NPA) as soon as it is classified as SMA-2, even before completing 90 days overdue.

  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(c) Statements 1, 2 and 4 are accurate per RBI's prudential framework: SMA-0 (up to 30 days), SMA-1 (31-60), SMA-2 (61-90 days overdue), and mandatory SMA reporting to CRILC. Statement 3 is wrong because NPA classification requires an account to remain overdue for more than 90 days, not automatic conversion at SMA-2 entry.

Q2 · Economy · Easy

The RBI raised the repo rate by 25 basis points this week, its first hike since February 2023. With reference to the repo rate as a monetary policy instrument, which one of the following best describes it?

  1. The rate at which RBI buys government securities outright from banks to permanently inject liquidity into the banking system.
  2. The rate at which commercial banks lend short-term funds to one another in the interbank call money market.
  3. The minimum rate below which banks are not permitted to lend to their most creditworthy borrowers.
  4. The rate at which RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks against government securities as collateral under a repurchase agreement, used to signal monetary policy stance.
Answer

(d) The repo rate is the rate at which RBI provides short-term liquidity to banks against government securities under a repurchase agreement, and changes in it are the primary tool for signalling and transmitting monetary policy stance. Option A describes an outright open market operation, B describes the call money rate, and C describes a lending rate floor like the MCLR, all of which are distinct instruments.

  2. 2 and 3 only

Q11 · Economy · Medium

The European Union's Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) has recently come under a US investigation. Which one of the following best describes the underlying mechanism of CBAM?

  1. It requires EU importers to declare the embedded emissions of covered imported goods and pay a carbon price equivalent to what domestic EU producers pay under the EU Emissions Trading System.
  2. It is a voluntary carbon-offset scheme under which exporting countries can buy carbon credits to reduce the tariff applicable to their goods.

(a) CBAM links the carbon price on imports to the price domestic EU producers pay under the EU Emissions Trading System, based on declared embedded emissions, as described in the article.
