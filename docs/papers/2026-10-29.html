The RBI Governor's call for States to pursue prudent borrowing draws attention to the constitutional and institutional framework governing State debt. Consider the following statements:

  1. Under Article 293 of the Constitution, a State government already indebted to the Union must obtain the Union's consent before raising further loans.
  2. The Fifteenth Finance Commission permits every State to breach the fiscal deficit ceiling of 3% of GSDP without any conditions attached.
  3. State Development Loans (SDLs) are securities issued by State governments and allotted through auctions conducted by the Reserve Bank of India as their debt manager.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3
Answer

(c) Statement 1 is correct per Article 293(3). Statement 3 is correct — SDLs are State liabilities, with RBI acting only as issuing/debt manager, not as an RBI obligation. Statement 2 is wrong: the extra borrowing space beyond 3% recommended by the Fifteenth Finance Commission was conditional on power-sector reforms, not unconditional.
