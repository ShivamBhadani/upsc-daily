  • Kakatiya architectural idiom: star-shaped platforms, black basalt (trikutalayam) sculpture, Dwarapala iconography and iconometry conventions
  • Legal-institutional framework: Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Sites and Remains Act, 1958 and ASI's conservation mandate
  • Conservation challenges: sourcing compatible stone, balancing authenticity with restoration, funding and skilled-craftsmen shortages
Mains 5 · GS-II · 15 marks

A Gram Sabha in a Fifth Schedule area recently invoked the Panchayats (Extension to Scheduled Areas) Act, 1996 to deny burial land to tribal Christians. Critically examine the scope of Gram Sabha powers under PESA vis-à-vis the Fundamental Rights guaranteed under Part III of the Constitution.

  • Constitutional basis: Article 244(1), Fifth Schedule, and objectives of PESA, 1996 in protecting tribal self-governance and customary institutions
  • Scope and limits of Gram Sabha powers under Section 4 of PESA (safeguarding customs, community resources, dispute resolution) versus Fundamental Rights such as Article 14, 21 and 25
  • Judicial and constitutional principle that subordinate/customary bodies cannot override Part III rights; balance between community autonomy and individual rights of minorities within tribal areas
