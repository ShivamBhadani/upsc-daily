Retail inflation breached the Reserve Bank of India's upper tolerance limit in October 2026. With reference to India's flexible inflation targeting framework, consider the following statements: How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. The flexible inflation targeting framework mandates the RBI to maintain annual retail inflation at 4%, with a tolerance band of plus or minus 2 percentage points.
  2. The inflation target under this framework is based on the Wholesale Price Index (WPI) rather than the Consumer Price Index (CPI).
  3. If CPI inflation remains outside the tolerance band for three consecutive quarters, the RBI is deemed to have failed to meet its statutory mandate.
  4. The Monetary Policy Committee that decides the policy repo rate has six members, three from the RBI (including the Governor as chairperson) and three external members appointed by the Central Government.
  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(c) Statements 1, 3 and 4 are correct: the target is 4% CPI inflation with a 2-6% band under Section 45ZA/45ZB of the RBI Act, breach for three consecutive quarters constitutes 'failure', and the MPC has the stated 3+3 composition. Statement 2 is wrong because the target is based on CPI (Combined), not WPI.

Consider the following statements regarding the pricing and taxation of petrol and diesel in India:

  1. Petrol and diesel prices in India were officially deregulated, allowing Oil Marketing Companies to revise retail prices based on global crude oil prices and other costs.
  2. Under the Administered Pricing Mechanism, the Union Government directly fixes the retail price of diesel and petrol every fortnight through a Cabinet order.

How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(c) Petrol was deregulated in 2010 and diesel in 2014, with OMCs setting prices on a market-linked, cost-plus basis, making statement 1 correct.
