  1. Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is the correct explanation for Statement-I
  2. Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct but Statement-II is not the correct explanation for Statement-I
  3. Statement-I is correct but Statement-II is incorrect

Answer

(a) Both statements are factually accurate: courts have held (e.g., Mansukhlal Vithaldas Chauhan v. Statement-II directly supplies that reasoning, so it explains Statement-I. The Kerala case, where the Governor acted on the Chief Minister's request independent of routine Cabinet advice, fits this established constitutional position.

  2. The Monetary Policy Committee is chaired by the Union Finance Secretary and comprises three officials of the Finance Ministry and three external members appointed by the RBI.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3
Answer

(c) Statement 1 and 3 are correct: the inflation target (4% +/-2%) is notified by the Government in consultation with the RBI for five-year blocks, and three-consecutive-quarter failure triggers a mandatory report to the Central Government under Section 45ZN of the RBI Act.   2. An imaging technique that uses magnetic fields and radio waves to generate detailed cross-sectional images of soft tissue, including blood vessels in the brain

With reference to trapped-ion quantum computing, a field recognised in this year's Infosys Prize for work on building programmable quantum systems using trapped ions, consider the following statements:

  1. Trapped-ion qubits are typically cooled to operating temperature using liquid-helium dilution refrigerators, the same technique used for superconducting qubits.
  2. Quantum superposition allows a qubit to represent the states 0 and 1 simultaneously, unlike a classical bit which can only be in one state at a time.
  3. Quantum entanglement produces correlations between qubits such that the measured state of one qubit is linked to the measured state of another entangled qubit, even when physically separated.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3
Answer

(b) Statement 1 is incorrect: trapped-ion qubits are confined by electromagnetic (Paul/RF) traps in ultra-high vacuum and cooled mainly through laser cooling, not through liquid-helium dilution refrigerators — that cryogenic method is characteristic of superconducting-circuit qubits, a competing quantum-computing architecture. Statements 2 and 3 correctly describe superposition and entanglement, the two defining quantum-mechanical properties exploited by qubits of any physical implementation.
