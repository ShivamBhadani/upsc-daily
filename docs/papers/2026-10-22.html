Q1 · Polity & Governance · Medium

The Supreme Court recently intervened in the conduct of Urban Local Body elections in Andhra Pradesh, directing the State Election Commission (SEC) to postpone the publication of the final candidate list. With reference to the constitutional position of State Election Commissions in India, consider the following statements: 1. The State Election Commission is constituted under Article 243K of the Constitution for elections to Panchayats and under Article 243ZA for Municipalities. 2. The State Election Commissioner can be removed from office only through impeachment by Parliament, similar to the procedure prescribed for the removal of the President. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer

(a) Statement 1 is correct: Article 243K constitutes the SEC for panchayat elections and Article 243ZA extends this to municipalities, making it a constitutional authority analogous to the ECI. Statement 2 is incorrect: Article 243K(2) provides that the State Election Commissioner is removable only in like manner and on like grounds as a High Court judge, not through presidential-style impeachment by Parliament.
