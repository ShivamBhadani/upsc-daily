Q1 · Economy · Medium

Consider the following statements regarding Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) in India: How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. FDI routed through the automatic route does not require prior approval of the Government or the RBI.
  2. FDI and Foreign Portfolio Investment (FPI) are governed under the same statutory sectoral cap and are treated as a single category of inflow.
  3. The Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) formulates India's FDI policy.
  4. Total FDI inflow comprises equity inflows along with reinvested earnings and other capital, of which equity is only one component.
  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(c) Statements 1, 3 and 4 are correct: automatic-route FDI needs no prior clearance, DPIIT (Ministry of Commerce and Industry) frames FDI policy, and standard BoP accounting splits total FDI into equity, reinvested earnings and other capital. Statement 2 is wrong — FDI and FPI are distinct investment categories with separate regulatory frameworks and caps, not a single combined one.

Q2 · Economy · Medium

Consider the following statements regarding the Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council: How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. The GST Council is a constitutional body established under Article 279A of the Constitution.
  2. Recommendations made by the GST Council are binding on both Parliament and the State legislatures.
  3. Compensation cess collected under GST is credited to a non-lapsable GST Compensation Fund meant to compensate States for revenue loss.
  4. Every decision of the GST Council must be taken by unanimous consent of all its members.
  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(b) Statements 1 and 3 are correct — the GST Council was created by the 101st Amendment under Article 279A, and compensation cess flows into a dedicated non-lapsable fund. Statement 2 is wrong since the Supreme Court in Mohit Minerals (2022) held Council recommendations have persuasive, not binding, value. Statement 4 is wrong because decisions need a three-fourths weighted majority of members present and voting, not unanimity.

With reference to Ways and Means Advances (WMA), consider the following statements: Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. WMA are temporary advances extended by the RBI to the Union and State Governments to help bridge mismatches between their receipts and payments.

Q9 · Economy · Medium

With reference to India's monetary policy framework amid an expected repo rate hike, consider:

  1. Statement-I: The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee operates under a flexible inflation targeting framework with an explicit numerical target.
  2. Statement-II: A rise in the policy repo rate raises the cost of funds for banks, which transmits to lending and deposit rates, thereby moderating aggregate demand and inflationary pressure.

  2. Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct but Statement-II is not the correct explanation for Statement-I

  4. Statement-I is incorrect but Statement-II is correct
Answer

(a) Since 2016, the RBI Act mandates flexible inflation targeting with a CPI target of 4%, within a band of +/-2%, operationalised by the Monetary Policy Committee (Statement-I correct). Q20 · Science & Technology · Medium

With reference to the event in Tuvalu where a marine heatwave wiped out almost all the coral that built the islands, which one of the following best describes the term 'coral bleaching'?

  1. It is the natural shedding of the outer calcium carbonate skeleton by corals during their annual reproductive cycle, unrelated to sea temperature.
  2. It is a bacterial infection transmitted by reef fish that dissolves the coral skeleton from within, independent of ocean temperature.
  3. It is the process by which corals absorb excess atmospheric carbon dioxide, lowering ocean pH and causing the coral skeleton to harden and turn white.
  4. It is the process by which corals expel the symbiotic algae (zooxanthellae) living in their tissues due to stress such as elevated sea temperatures, causing them to turn white and often die.
Answer

(d) Coral bleaching occurs when thermal stress from events like marine heatwaves causes corals to expel their symbiotic zooxanthellae, which provide both colour and nutrition, leaving the coral skeleton visible and the organism vulnerable to death — exactly what the article describes happening to Tuvalu's reef. The other options describe unrelated or fictitious processes: annual skeletal shedding and fish-borne bacterial dissolution do not exist, and CO2 absorption lowering pH describes ocean acidification, a distinct process from bleaching.

Tests the biological mechanism of coral bleaching versus ocean acidification, a distinction UPSC has tested in prior environment-science questions.
