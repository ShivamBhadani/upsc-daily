Q1 · Economy · Medium

The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee recently raised the policy repo rate, adjusting other rates in the liquidity adjustment facility corridor. Consider the following statements regarding RBI's rate instruments and the Monetary Policy Committee. How many of the statements given above are correct?

  1. The policy repo rate is the rate at which RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks against the collateral of government securities.
  2. The Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) requires banks to pledge government securities as collateral when depositing surplus funds with RBI.
  3. The Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) allows banks to borrow overnight funds from RBI at a rate higher than the repo rate, acting as a penal-rate liquidity window.
  4. The Monetary Policy Committee consists of six members, three from the RBI and three experts appointed by the Central Government.
  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(c) Statement 2 is false: the SDF, introduced in April 2022 to replace the fixed-rate reverse repo, is a collateral-free facility to absorb excess liquidity — that collateral-free feature is precisely why it was introduced. Statements 1, 3 and 4 are correct: repo lending is against G-secs, MSF sits above repo as a penal overnight window, and the MPC under the RBI Act, 1934 has three RBI members and three government-appointed experts.
