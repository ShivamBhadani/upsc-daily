Q1 · International Relations · Medium

Consider the following statements regarding the WHO Pandemic Agreement (PA) adopted in 2025. How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. The Pandemic Agreement was adopted by the World Health Assembly exercising its treaty-making power under Article 19 of the WHO Constitution.
  2. The Agreement enters into force only after ratification by every member-state of the WHO.
  3. The Pathogen Access and Benefit-Sharing (PABS) annex to the Agreement has already been finalised and ratified by member-states.
  4. India voted against the adoption of the Pandemic Agreement at the World Health Assembly.
  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(a) Only statement 1 is correct: the WHA adopts conventions under Article 19 of the WHO Constitution. International treaties of this kind typically need ratification by a specified threshold of states (not all members) to enter into force, and the PABS annex is explicitly still under negotiation, not finalised. The article states no country voted against the PA; India was among the 124 adopting states.

Tests WHO's constitutional treaty-making power and the ratification process for international conventions, akin to UPSC's recurring questions on WHO/IHR institutional structure.

Q2 · Economy · Easy

With reference to the Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) and the repo rate, consider the following statements. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. The Monetary Policy Committee consists of six members, three from the RBI and three external members appointed by the Central Government.
  2. The repo rate is the rate at which the RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks against the collateral of government securities.
  3. A revised repo rate takes effect for retail loans only after it is ratified by Parliament.
  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only

Answer

(a) Statements 1 and 2 are correct and describe the statutory composition of the MPC under the RBI Act, 1934 (as amended) and the standard definition of the repo rate. Statement 3 is false — retail and small-business loans are automatically linked to an external benchmark (such as the repo rate) under RBI guidelines, and no parliamentary ratification is required for rate transmission.

Q3 · Economy · Medium

Consider the following statements regarding India's foreign exchange reserves. How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. India's foreign exchange reserves comprise Foreign Currency Assets, gold, Special Drawing Rights, and the Reserve Tranche Position in the IMF.
  2. Special Drawing Rights (SDR) is a currency issued by the IMF that circulates alongside national currencies.
  3. The Reserve Tranche Position represents the portion of India's IMF quota that India can draw upon without conditionality.

  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four

(b) Statements 1 and 3 are correct — these are the standard four components of reserves and the correct description of the Reserve Tranche Position. Q5 · Science & Technology · Medium

With reference to India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, which one of the following best describes a Data Fiduciary's obligation while processing the personal data of a child?
