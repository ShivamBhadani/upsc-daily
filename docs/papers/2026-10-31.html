Q1 · Economy · Medium

India's fiscal deficit for April-September FY27 has widened to 42.1% of the full-year target. In this context, consider the following statements regarding fiscal deficit and related concepts under India's fiscal framework:

  1. Fiscal deficit refers to the excess of total expenditure over total receipts excluding borrowings.
  2. The FRBM Act, 2003 fixes the fiscal deficit target at 3% of GDP for the Union Government with no provision for deviation under any circumstances.
  3. Revenue deficit is the excess of revenue expenditure over revenue receipts.
  4. Effective revenue deficit excludes grants given for creation of capital assets from the revenue deficit.

How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(c) Statements 1, 3 and 4 correctly define fiscal deficit, revenue deficit and effective revenue deficit respectively. Statement 2 is wrong because the FRBM Act contains an 'escape clause' (Section 4(2)) permitting deviation from the target during national security, calamity or severe economic stress.

Tests FRBM Act provisions and deficit definitions; similar to UPSC 2015/2016 prelims questions distinguishing fiscal, revenue, primary and effective revenue deficit.

Q3 · Economy · Medium

Q7 · Economy · Hard

Q8 · Science & Technology · Medium
