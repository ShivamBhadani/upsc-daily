Q1 · Economy · Medium

Consider the following statements regarding the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999, in the context of the RBI's recent clarifications on forex hedging and reserve norms. How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. The Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve is maintained by authorised dealer banks with the RBI under powers granted by the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999.
  2. Authorised dealer banks are permitted to deal in foreign exchange only after obtaining a licence from the RBI under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.
  3. Current account transactions under FEMA are, as a general rule, freely permitted unless specifically restricted by rules framed by the Central Government.
  4. The Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 replaced the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act (FERA), 1973.
  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(c) Statements 1, 3 and 4 are correct: RBI's FERR directions and AD bank licensing both flow from FEMA, current account transactions are presumptively free unless restricted, and FEMA 1999 replaced the stricter FERA 1973. Statement 2 is wrong because AD bank authorisation to deal in forex is granted under Section 10 of FEMA, not the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, which governs banking business generally, not foreign exchange dealing specifically.

Tests FEMA 1999 vs FERA 1973 and AD bank licensing basics, similar to UPSC's recurring questions on capital/current account transaction classification under FEMA.

  1. Securities Transaction Tax (STT) is a direct tax levied on transactions in listed securities and is administered by the Income Tax Department.
  2. Corporate tax is levied on the income of domestic and foreign companies operating in India under the Income-tax Act, 1961.

  4. Net direct tax collection is computed by adding refunds issued to the gross direct tax collected.

  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(b) Statements 1 and 2 are correct. With reference to India's September trade data showing a widened trade gap, which one of the following best describes the term 'merchandise trade deficit'?

Q11 · Science & Technology · Medium

With reference to artificial intelligence, which one of the following best describes an 'open-weight' model, such as the one recently released by Meta?
