Q1 · Economy · Medium

The Union government's H1 FY27 finances were supported by dividends, profits and disinvestment receipts even as tax collections remained subdued. In this context, consider the following statements. How many of the statements given above are correct?

  1. Fiscal deficit is the difference between the government's total expenditure and its total receipts excluding borrowings.
  2. Dividends and profits received from the RBI and public sector undertakings are classified as capital receipts in the Union Budget.
  3. Proceeds from disinvestment of government equity in public sector enterprises are classified as capital receipts.
  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. All three
  4. None
Answer

(b) Statement 1 is the standard definition of fiscal deficit. Statement 3 is correct — disinvestment proceeds are capital receipts since they reduce government assets/equity. Statement 2 is wrong: RBI/PSU dividends and profits are non-tax revenue receipts, not capital receipts, since they don't create a liability or reduce an asset.

Q3 · Science & Technology · Easy

With reference to cloud seeding, which one of the following best describes the technique and a key limitation associated with it, as seen in Delhi's trials?

  1. It involves dispersing substances such as silver iodide or sodium chloride into clouds with sufficient existing moisture to induce precipitation, and its success depends on pre-existing favourable cloud conditions rather than guaranteeing rain in dry weather.
  2. It involves releasing sulphate aerosols into the stratosphere to reflect solar radiation and lower global temperatures, a technique also known as solar radiation management.
  3. It involves injecting carbon dioxide into underground saline aquifers for permanent sequestration, primarily used to cut industrial emissions.
  4. It involves releasing biodegradable polymers into water bodies to absorb pollutants, commonly used to counter eutrophication.
Answer

(a) Cloud seeding introduces nucleating agents into clouds that already have adequate moisture to trigger precipitation; it cannot create rain from dry skies, which is why Delhi's October 2025 trial produced little to no rainfall. Options 2, 3 and 4 describe unrelated techniques — solar radiation management, carbon capture and storage, and water pollutant remediation respectively.

Tests static science concept of weather modification technology, distinct from geoengineering/CCS techniques often confused with it.
