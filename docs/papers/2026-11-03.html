Q1 · Economy · Medium

The Union Finance Minister recently stressed capital expenditure (capex) as central to India's growth strategy while maintaining fiscal prudence. With reference to government expenditure and fiscal management in India, consider the following statements:

  1. Capital expenditure is expenditure that creates long-term physical or financial assets for the government.
  2. Revenue expenditure incurred by the government is normally non-recurring in nature.
  3. The FRBM Act, 2003 mandates the Central Government to eliminate the revenue deficit completely in every financial year without exception.
  4. Capital expenditure is generally considered to have a higher fiscal multiplier effect on economic growth than revenue expenditure.

How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(b) Statements 1 and 4 are correct: capex builds durable assets (roads, ports, defence equipment) and is widely held by the RBI and Finance Ministry to have a stronger growth multiplier than revenue spending. Statement 2 is wrong because revenue expenditure (salaries, subsidies, interest payments) is precisely the recurring component of the budget. Statement 3 is wrong because the FRBM framework has been amended multiple times and currently emphasises a fiscal deficit glide path with an 'escape clause', not an absolute annual elimination of revenue deficit.
