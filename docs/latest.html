  2. The current base year of the WPI series is 2011-12.
  3. Unlike the Consumer Price Index, the WPI basket also carries a weight for services such as housing and education.
  4. Fuel and power constitutes a separate major group in the WPI basket, distinct from primary articles and manufactured products.

  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(c) Statements 1, 2 and 4 are correct: WPI is compiled by the Office of the Economic Adviser (DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce and Industry) on a 2011-12 base, with Fuel & Power as a distinct major group alongside Primary Articles and Manufactured Products. Statement 3 is false — WPI measures wholesale/producer prices of goods only and excludes services, which is precisely why CPI (which includes housing, education, health) is used for inflation targeting instead.

Q2 · Science & Technology · Hard

With reference to quantum computing, in the context of Microsoft's new 'topological' quantum chip, consider the following statements: How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. A qubit can exist in a superposition of the 0 and 1 states simultaneously, unlike a classical bit.
  2. The Majorana fermion is a hypothetical particle that acts as its own antiparticle, and is relevant to topological quantum computing.
  3. A topological qubit stores quantum information primarily using the spin orientation of a single trapped ion held in an electromagnetic field.
  4. Once a company's press release describes a technological claim in detail, peer review of the underlying scientific paper becomes unnecessary.
  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(b) Statements 1 and 2 are correct — superposition is the defining property of a qubit, and the Majorana fermion (its own antiparticle) underlies the non-abelian anyon states topological computing exploits. Statement 3 is false — storing information via a trapped ion's spin describes trapped-ion quantum computing, a different and competing approach, not the topological route. Statement 4 is false and contradicts the article's own point: the gap between Microsoft's press release and its more circumspect peer-reviewed paper is exactly why independent verification matters.

Tests basic concepts of qubits and quantum computing approaches, an emerging area UPSC has begun probing (cf. UPSC 2023 question on quantum computing applications).

Q3 · Polity & Governance · Easy

With reference to the GST Council, whose 57th meeting recently cleared farm-related tax concessions, consider the following statements: Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. The GST Council is a constitutional body established under Article 279A of the Constitution of India.
  2. Every decision of the GST Council must be taken by a unanimous vote of all its members, as mandated by the Constitution.
  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer

(a) Statement 1 is correct — the GST Council was constituted by the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016 under Article 279A. Statement 2 is incorrect — decisions are taken by a majority of not less than three-fourths of the weighted votes cast, with the Centre's vote weighted at one-third and all states' votes combined weighted at two-thirds; unanimity is not constitutionally required.

Classic Article 279A/GST Council composition and voting-weightage question, a recurring UPSC favourite (cf. UPSC 2017, 2019 on GST Council powers).

With reference to disinvestment of Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs) in India, in the context of the Centre nearing its Rs 80,000 crore disinvestment target via the IDBI Bank stake sale, consider the following statements: Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. The Department of Investment and Public Asset Management (DIPAM) is the nodal department for disinvestment of CPSEs under the Ministry of Finance.
  2. Strategic disinvestment, by definition, requires the government to retain at least 51% equity and management control in the CPSE after the transaction.

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only

Answer

(c) Statements 1 and 3 are correct: DIPAM under the Finance Ministry steers CPSE disinvestment, and proceeds flow into the Public Account (via the National Investment Fund mechanism merged into it in 2013) and can fund capital expenditure. Q6 · Science & Technology · Hard

With reference to recent developments in quantum computing, which one of the following best describes a 'topological qubit'?

  1. A qubit built using a superconducting loop cooled to near absolute zero, where information is stored in the direction of current flow
  2. A qubit realised by trapping a single ion in an electromagnetic field and manipulating its energy levels with lasers
  3. A qubit that uses the polarization state of a single photon to represent quantum information for secure communication
  4. A qubit that encodes information in the non-local braiding properties of quasiparticles such as Majorana zero modes, making it intrinsically more resistant to local environmental noise
Answer

(d) A topological qubit, as pursued by Microsoft's 'topoconductor' material, stores information in the non-local topological properties of quasiparticles (Majorana zero modes), which in principle makes the stored information harder to disturb through local noise. Options (a) and (b) describe superconducting and trapped-ion qubits respectively — rival, non-topological approaches — while option (c) describes photonic qubits used mainly in quantum communication, not topological computing.

Q7 · Economy · Medium

Consider the following two statements in the context of India's external sector challenges: Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?

  1. Statement-I: India's current account deficit is structurally driven to a large extent by its import dependence on oil, gas and gold.
  2. Statement-II: A widening current account deficit increases downward pressure on the domestic currency by raising the net demand for foreign exchange.
  1. Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is the correct explanation for Statement-I
  2. Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct but Statement-II is not the correct explanation for Statement-I
  3. Statement-I is correct but Statement-II is incorrect

Answer

(b) Both statements are independently correct — India's CAD is heavily a function of oil, gas and gold imports, as noted by BCG's Janmejaya Sinha, and a wider CAD does raise forex demand that pressures the rupee. However, Statement-II explains a consequence of a widening CAD on the currency, not the cause of why the CAD itself is structurally driven by energy and gold imports — so it is not the correct explanation for Statement-I.

Q9 · Polity & Governance · Medium

The appointment of a State Governor has again drawn public protest. Consider the following statements on the constitutional office of Governor. How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. The Governor of a State is appointed by the President under Article 155 of the Constitution.
  2. The Constitution requires a person to be a permanent resident of the State concerned to be eligible for appointment as its Governor.
  3. A Governor holds office during the pleasure of the President, notwithstanding the five-year term prescribed under Article 156.
  4. Unlike the President, a Governor cannot address the State Legislature at the commencement of its first session after a general election.
  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(a) Only statement 1 is correct — the President appoints the Governor under Article 155. Statement 2 is wrong because the Constitution prescribes no residency requirement (only citizenship and minimum age of 35 under Article 157); by convention, Governors are usually appointed from outside the State. Statement 3 is a trick reversal — the pleasure doctrine and the five-year term coexist, not 'notwithstanding' one another, but more importantly statement 4 is simply false since Article 176 obliges the Governor to address the Legislature, just as the President addresses Parliament under Article 87.

Tests Articles 155-158 and 176 on the Governor's appointment, tenure and functions; similar to recurring UPSC questions on gubernatorial powers and conventions.

Q13 · Environment & Ecology · Medium

The Jeypore ground gecko was first described in 1878, presumed extinct for roughly 130 years, and then rediscovered in 2010 in Odisha. Which one of the following best describes a species of this kind in conservation biology?

  1. A 'Lazarus taxon' — a species that reappears in the observational or fossil record after a long period during which it was believed extinct
  2. A species that was never formally described in scientific literature prior to its rediscovery
  3. A species classified as 'Extinct in the Wild' because only captive-bred populations had survived during the interim period
  4. A species that, being confined to protected areas, is ineligible for assessment under the IUCN Red List
Answer

(a) Such species are termed 'Lazarus taxa' in conservation biology — organisms rediscovered after being presumed extinct for an extended period, exactly matching the gecko's history of an 1878 description followed by a 2010 rediscovery. It was formally described well before its rediscovery (ruling out option 2), no captive population was involved (ruling out option 3), and confinement to a habitat does not exempt a species from Red List assessment (ruling out option 4).

Tests the conservation-biology concept of 'Lazarus taxon' and rediscovered species; mirrors UPSC's 'best describes the term' format (e.g., 2019 Merchant Discount Rate question).
