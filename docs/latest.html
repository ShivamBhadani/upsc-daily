Q1 · Economy · Medium

With reference to India's monetary policy framework, in the context of retail inflation recently crossing the RBI's upper tolerance limit, consider the following statements: How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. The Reserve Bank of India's inflation target under the flexible inflation targeting framework is based on the Consumer Price Index (Combined), not the Wholesale Price Index.
  2. The current tolerance band permits retail inflation to range between 2% and 6%, with 6% as the upper limit.
  3. The six-member Monetary Policy Committee has a majority of four members appointed by the Government of India from outside the RBI.
  4. If retail inflation remains outside the tolerance band for three consecutive quarters, the RBI Governor is statutorily required to resign.
  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(b) Statements 1 and 2 are correct: RBI's flexible inflation targeting under Section 45ZA of the RBI Act uses CPI (Combined) with a 2-6% band. Statement 3 is wrong since the MPC is evenly split 3-3 between RBI members and government appointees, not a four-member external majority. Statement 4 is wrong because missing the band for three consecutive quarters only triggers a report to the Government explaining reasons and remedial timelines, not a resignation.

Tests RBI's flexible inflation targeting framework (Section 45ZA, RBI Act); cf. UPSC 2021 Prelims question on expansionary monetary policy actions.
