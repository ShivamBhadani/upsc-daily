Q1 · Economy · Medium

The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) recently raised the repo rate and shifted its policy stance. With reference to the MPC, consider the following statements: How many of the above statements are correct?

  1. The Monetary Policy Committee of the RBI comprises six members, with the RBI Governor serving as its Chairperson.
  2. Of the six MPC members, three are external members appointed by the Central Government.
  3. The repo rate decided by the MPC is the rate at which the RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks against government securities.
  4. Decisions of the MPC require the unanimous consent of all six members before they can be implemented.
  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(c) Statements 1-3 are correct statutory and functional facts about the MPC under Section 45ZB of the RBI Act. Statement 4 is wrong: MPC decisions are taken by majority vote, with the Governor holding a casting vote in case of a tie, not by unanimity — even though the October hike happened to be unanimous.

  1. Curtailment refers to the deliberate reduction of power generation from a renewable energy plant below its available potential, usually due to grid or transmission constraints.
  2. Under the Indian Electricity Grid Code, grid frequency must be maintained within a wide band of 48-52 Hz at all times.
  3. Under the Renewable Purchase Obligation framework, electricity distribution licensees are required to source a minimum proportion of their power from renewable energy sources.
  4. Solar power is classified as a 'dispatchable' source of electricity since its output can be precisely scheduled to match demand at any given time.
  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(b) Statements 1 and 3 correctly describe curtailment and the Renewable Purchase Obligation (RPO) mechanism under the Electricity Act, 2003.
