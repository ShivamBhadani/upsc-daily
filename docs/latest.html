Q1 · Economy · Medium

India recently completed a decade of 'flexible inflation targeting' as the formal monetary policy framework of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). Consider the following statements regarding this framework. How many of the statements given above are correct?

  1. Under the framework, the RBI is mandated to maintain Consumer Price Index inflation at 4% with a tolerance band of +/- 2 percentage points.
  2. The Monetary Policy Committee that decides the policy repo rate has six members, with the RBI Governor as chairperson.
  3. The flexible inflation targeting framework was incorporated through an amendment to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
  4. Decisions of the Monetary Policy Committee are taken only by unanimous consensus and cannot be put to a vote.
  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four
Answer

(c) Statements 1-3 are correct: the 4%+/-2% CPI target, the six-member MPC chaired by the Governor, and the 2016 amendment to Section 45ZB of the RBI Act, 1934 are all accurate. Statement 4 is false — MPC decisions are taken by majority vote, each member gets one vote, and the Governor holds a casting vote in case of a tie.

Tests Monetary Policy Committee composition and the inflation-targeting mandate, similar to UPSC 2017 Prelims question on the Monetary Policy Committee of RBI.

Answer

Q6 · Geography · Easy

Rising crude oil prices have been linked to the closure of the Strait of Hormuz. With reference to the Strait of Hormuz, which one of the following best describes it?

  1. A canal connecting the Mediterranean Sea with the Red Sea, critical for oil tanker traffic between Europe and Asia
  2. A strait connecting the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman, through which a large share of the world's seaborne crude oil exports transits

  4. A strait connecting the landlocked Caspian Sea with the Persian Gulf, used primarily for natural gas transport
Answer

(b) The Strait of Hormuz links the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea, and is the key chokepoint for Gulf crude oil exports, which is why its closure (as in the article) spikes global oil prices. Q13 · Geography · Easy

With reference to the heavy rainfall and waterlogging recently witnessed in Chennai and other parts of Tamil Nadu in October, which one of the following best describes the meteorological phenomenon primarily responsible for such rainfall during this period?

  1. It is caused by the Southwest Monsoon winds, which continue to bring rainfall to Tamil Nadu until December before fully retreating.

Q18 · Science & Technology · Easy

With reference to pulses research being pushed under India's National Mission on Pulses, which one of the following best describes the key agronomic significance of leguminous pulse crops in a cropping system?

  1. They are crops developed exclusively through genetic modification to resist pod borer pests.
  2. They fix atmospheric nitrogen in the soil through a symbiotic relationship with Rhizobium bacteria in their root nodules, improving soil fertility.

Answer

(b) Pulses are leguminous crops whose root nodules host Rhizobium bacteria that convert atmospheric nitrogen into usable forms, enriching soil fertility — this is why they are promoted in crop rotation and intercropping.
